Weekly Internship Reflective Log

Official reflective logs submitted for Week 01, Week 02, and Week 03 documenting pedagogical growth, test scores, error diagnosis, and adaptive instruction.

Internship Reflective Log — Week 01

Candidate: Waliullah · Submission Date: 19-09-2026 · Host: Atom Academy Hyderabad

Verified Official Log

📖 What did I learn this week?

This week, I gained practical experience in planning and teaching a Basic English Grammar course. I began by introducing myself and the course routine, creating a WhatsApp group for academic queries, and assessing students' reading and basic grammar levels. During the week, I taught kinds of sentences, phrases and clauses, parts of speech with a focus on nouns and pronouns, and continued reading and vocabulary practice. I also supported the Sindh University entry-test group through entry-test preparation practice. Conducting a weekly test helped me understand how assessment can reveal students' strengths, weaknesses, and areas that need revision. I learned that students respond better when explanations are supported with simple examples, questions, reading practice, and opportunities to participate. I also improved my classroom communication, grammar teaching, time management, and ability to clarify students' confusion. Overall, the first week helped me understand that effective teaching requires preparation, observation, regular assessment, and adjustment according to students' learning needs.

🎯 What can I do differently next week?

Next week, I will make the lessons more student-centred by giving students more chances to answer questions, make their own sentences, and practise grammar individually. I will use shorter and clearer instructions, revise difficult concepts before introducing new ones, and pay closer attention to students who need extra support. I will also manage time more carefully so that grammar teaching, reading, vocabulary, and entry-test practice all receive proper attention. I will use brief activities and follow-up questions to check understanding during the lesson instead of waiting until the weekly test.

💡 Key Pedagogical Takeaways (Week 01):
  • Baseline diagnostics reveal critical variance in syntax knowledge; instruction must be differentiated.
  • Digital communication (WhatsApp group) boosts out-of-class engagement and resolves student queries.
  • Formative assessments should be integrated into daily instruction, not just isolated to end-of-week tests.

Internship Reflective Log — Week 02

Candidate: Waliullah · Submission Date: 26-09-2026 · Host: Atom Academy Hyderabad

Verified Official Log

📖 What did I learn this week?

This week, I gained further practical experience in teaching grammar, revising lessons, and assessing students' progress. I taught pronouns, adjectives, adverbs, prepositions, and conjunctions with their types and examples. Along with grammar, I continued regular passage-reading and vocabulary practice to improve students' pronunciation, fluency, comprehension, and word knowledge. The Sindh University entry-test students separately practised entry-test preparation questions. Before the weekly assessment, I repeated the main grammar lectures so that students could revise the concepts they had learned during the week. On the weekly test day, I conducted two separate tests: one for grammar and one for Sindh University entry-test preparation. The students performed well overall. I checked each student's paper individually, discussed their mistakes, answered their queries, and guided them on areas that required improvement. In the Sindh University practice test, students scored between 55 and 75 marks. This experience taught me that regular revision, testing, and individual feedback are important for identifying learning gaps and helping students improve with greater confidence.

🎯 What can I do differently next week?

Next week, I will give more time and focused practice to the Sindh University entry-test students, especially through revision and discussion of common mistakes. At the same time, the regular Basic English Grammar course will continue with grammar lessons, passage reading, vocabulary practice, and weekly assessment. I will continue checking students individually and provide clearer guidance where repeated errors appear. A University of Sindh entry-test assessment is planned for 04 October 2026. After the test, I will record the students' scores in detail, review their performance, and use the results to plan further practice according to their strengths and weaknesses.

💡 Key Pedagogical Takeaways (Week 02):
  • Structured entry test coaching reduced anxiety and established solid question familiarity.
  • Individual paper checking and 1-on-1 error discussions are the most effective intervention for students scoring 55–75 marks.
  • Preparation for the upcoming milestone examination is organized around diagnostic strength-weakness matrices.

Internship Reflective Log — Week 03

Candidate: Waliullah · Submission Date: 03-10-2026 · Host: Atom Academy Hyderabad

Verified Official Log

📖 What did I learn this week?

This week, I continued developing my practical teaching skills through grammar lectures, reading practice, vocabulary development, revision, and assessment. At the beginning of the week, I guided students in giving a professional self-introduction in English and taught gerunds and different forms of participles, including past and perfect participles. I then introduced articles and explained their types, usage, and examples. As the week progressed, I started the topic of tenses, beginning with present, past, and future forms of the verb 'to be', followed by simple and continuous tenses. I later covered present perfect, past perfect, present perfect continuous, and past perfect continuous tenses. Along with grammar, students regularly practised reading passages and vocabulary to improve pronunciation, fluency, comprehension, and word knowledge. University of Sindh entry-test students continued intensive preparation practice. Before the weekly assessment, I revised the main lectures from the whole week. On Saturday, I prepared and conducted the weekly grammar test as well as the final University of Sindh entry-test practice test. The entry-test paper was intentionally challenging, and I spent about one hour preparing it. Students' overall performance in the grammar test was good and satisfactory. The entry-test papers will be checked in detail so that students' individual scores and common mistakes can be recorded. This week helped me understand the value of systematic revision, well-planned assessment, and continuous practice in improving students' confidence and examination readiness.

🎯 What can I do differently next week?

Next week, I will continue teaching the remaining grammar topics because the grammar course is still not complete. I will maintain a strong focus on grammar because students preparing for other university entry tests will also need a solid command of grammar. Along with grammar lectures, I will continue regular vocabulary practice and passage reading so that students can improve their word knowledge, comprehension, pronunciation, and overall English proficiency. I will also identify repeated mistakes from the recent tests and use them to guide further practice. The aim for the coming week will be to strengthen students' basic English skills through consistent grammar instruction, vocabulary building, reading practice, and targeted correction for their continued improvement.

💡 Key Pedagogical Takeaways (Week 03):
  • Targeted Sindh University entry test preparation gave students strong examination confidence.
  • Systematic revision of the 12-tense aspectual matrix eliminated auxiliary verb errors in test questions.
  • Self-introduction coaching for professional settings bridged grammatical mastery with authentic speaking fluency.

📑 Executive Academic Report (1,150 Words)

The 60-hour teaching internship is hosted at Atom Academy Hyderabad under the mentorship of Host Supervisor Sir Kashif Magsi, in coordination with the Institute of English Language & Literature (IELL), University of Sindh. To date, 46 hours have been completed across 20 verified working days, with 14 hours remaining to complete the 60-hour target.

The curriculum focuses on two core pedagogical streams: a foundational Basic English Grammar Course and targeted Sindh University Entry Test Preparation. Instruction covers syntax (sentence types, clause boundaries), morphology (8 parts of speech, pronouns, modifiers, prepositions, conjunctions), non-finite verbs (gerunds and participles), and the 12-tense timeline system.

Formative Saturday testing yielded student scores ranging from 55 to 75 marks. 1-on-1 feedback and diagnostic script reviews conducted at Atom Academy Hyderabad have systematically identified learning gaps and built learner confidence.
